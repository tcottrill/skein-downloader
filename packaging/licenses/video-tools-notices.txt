Skein Downloader: bundled video tools and where to get their source

Skein Downloader is GPL-3.0-or-later. Its Flatpak and AppImage also ship these programs,
unmodified, under their own licenses. The source archives named below are published
with every Skein Downloader release, next to the packages:
https://github.com/tcottrill/skein-downloader/releases

yt-dlp 2026.08.19, official yt-dlp_linux executable
  https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/releases/tag/2026.08.19
  License: Unlicense (yt-dlp-LICENSE.txt).
  Source: yt-dlp-2026.08.19-source.tar.gz, the upstream yt-dlp.tar.gz of that release.
  The standalone executable also contains Python and other components with their own
  licenses, some GPL (yt-dlp-THIRD-PARTY.txt). yt-dlp's maintainers provide those
  sources from the original projects or on request to maintainers@yt-dlp.org.

Deno v2.9.8, official x86_64 Linux executable
  https://github.com/denoland/deno/releases/tag/v2.9.8
  License: MIT (deno-LICENSE.md).
  Source: deno-v2.9.8-source.tar.gz, the upstream deno_src.tar.gz of that release.

FFmpeg and ffprobe
  Flatpak: provided by the org.freedesktop.Platform 24.08 runtime, not by this package.
  Their sources and notices come from the Freedesktop runtime distributors.
  AppImage: a static GPL build from yt-dlp/FFmpeg-Builds (ffmpeg-LICENSE.txt).
  ffmpeg-source-manifest.txt lists the exact FFmpeg commit, build recipe, toolchain and
  every library revision it was built from, all in public repositories. It is inside
  the AppImage (usr/share/licenses/skein-downloader) and published beside it.

wxWidgets 3.2.12 is linked into the program (wxWindows Library Licence, wxWidgets.txt);
its source is https://github.com/wxWidgets/wxWidgets/tree/v3.2.12.
